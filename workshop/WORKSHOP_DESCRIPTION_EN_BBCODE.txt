[h1]Slay the Spire 2 - Shin Getter Mod[/h1]

[i]Three hearts. One Getter. Endless evolution.[/i]

Shin Getter crosses space and time to climb the Spire. Shift between Shin Getter 1's explosive offense, Shin Getter 2's high-speed tactics, and Shin Getter 3's iron defense; then awaken Shin Getter Dragon and let the Getter Rays consume the tower.

[h2]⚡ Combat at a glance[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Four forms, one character.[/b] Shin Getter 1 focuses on Vigor bursts. Shin Getter 2 gains extra Energy and draw while its Block is halved. Shin Getter 3 holds the line with Plating and defensive retaliation. Shin Getter Dragon accepts rewards from all three forms.
[*][b]Transformation is a decision.[/b] Form-specific cards highlight the strongest actions available now. Movement, transformation cards, and related relics let you make shifting part of an attacking turn.
[*][b]A complete character experience.[/b] Custom card frames, character animation, VFX, voices, finishing music, an exclusive event, and Ancient dialogue are all included.
[/list]

[h2]🧭 Starter routes[/h2]

[list]
[*][b]Vigor burst:[/b] Build Vigor, then finish with cards such as [i]Valor[/i] and [i]Dive Strike[/i].
[*][b]Exhaust loop:[/b] Use exhausting cards to trigger [i]Getter Claw[/i]'s free damage. [i]Parts Swap[/i] recovers key pieces, while Shin Getter 2 can further amplify the payoff.
[*][b]Iron retaliation:[/b] Stack Plating and Block in Shin Getter 3, then turn the enemy turn into a chance to counterattack.
[*][b]Transformation chain:[/b] Build around frequent shifting, movement-triggered transformations, and [i]Chosen One[/i] so each change becomes resources or defense.
[/list]

[h2]🚀 How to play[/h2]

[list]
[*]After subscribing, start the game and open [b]Settings → Mod Settings[/b] from the main menu.
[*]Find “真盖塔模组” (Shin Getter Mod) under [b]Installed Mods[/b], enable it, and accept the load prompt after confirming the source is trusted.
[*]Fully quit and relaunch the game. Enabling or disabling a mod takes effect after a restart.
[*]Start a new run and select [b]Shin Getter[/b].
[/list]

On your first run, follow the highlighted form-specific cards to establish a rhythm, then branch into one of the routes above.
