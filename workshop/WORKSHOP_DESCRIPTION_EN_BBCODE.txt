[h1]Slay the Spire 2 - Shin Getter Mod[/h1]

Pilot Shin Getter up the Spire, weaving transformations into offense and defense.

[h2]Four forms, your build[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shin Getter 1:[/b] Build Vigor for explosive attacks.
[*][b]Shin Getter 2:[/b] Trade halved Block for extra Energy and card draw.
[*][b]Shin Getter 3:[/b] Rely on Plating, Block, and defensive retaliation.
[*][b]Shin Getter Dragon:[/b] Accept rewards from all three forms and combine their strengths.
[/list]
Build around Vigor bursts, Exhaust loops, defensive retaliation, or transformation chains with exclusive relics, potions, and enchantments.

[h2]The Getter team's journey[/h2]
Discover unique event branches and Getter Mandala. In standard single-player Shin Getter runs, develop persistent bonds between Ryoma, Hayato, Benkei, and the Ancients. Form animations, attack effects, character voices, and selectable BGM bring Getter's combat style to life.

77 cards, 13 relics, 6 potions, and 2 enchantments. Available in Simplified Chinese, English, and Japanese.

[h2]Installation and compatibility[/h2]
For game [b]0.111 Beta[/b]. Never mix stable and Beta files; fully restart the game after switching.
blacksouls2 is optional: Getter Mandala can appear in SmokeForest, and Ancient bonds support its statistics modifier. BaseLib is not required by this mod. If using BaseLib, QuickRestart, or dependent mods, choose versions compatible with 0.111.
