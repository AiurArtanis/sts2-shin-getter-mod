[h1]Slay the Spire 2 - Shin Getter Mod[/h1]

Pilot Shin Getter up the Spire. Switch between four forms and make transformation part of each turn's offense and defense.

[h2]Four forms, one deck[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Shin Getter 1:[/b] Build Vigor for powerful bursts of damage.
[*][b]Shin Getter 2:[/b] Gain extra Energy and card draw at the cost of halved Block.
[*][b]Shin Getter 3:[/b] Hold the line with Plating, Block, and defensive retaliation.
[*][b]Shin Getter Dragon:[/b] Accept rewards from all three forms and combine their strengths.
[/list]

[h2]Choose your build[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Vigor burst:[/b] Build Vigor, then finish with a powerful attack.
[*][b]Exhaust loop:[/b] Exhaust cards to trigger Getter Claw, and use Parts Swap to recover key cards.
[*][b]Iron retaliation:[/b] Stack Plating and Block to turn enemy attacks into counterattack opportunities.
[*][b]Transformation chain:[/b] Build around transformation cards and related relics to turn form changes into resources or defense.
[/list]

[h2]Beyond combat[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Event invasions:[/b] Discover Getter-team branches in familiar Spire events, plus the Getter Mandala event.
[*][b]Ancient bonds:[/b] Progress through dialogue and bonds between the three-pilot team and the Ancients in standard single-player Shin Getter runs, without changing the original events' combat, rewards, or scoring.
[*][b]Battle presentation:[/b] Form animations, attack effects, character voices, and selectable BGM bring Getter's combat style to life.
[/list]

Includes 77 cards, 13 relics, 6 potions, and 2 enchantments. Available in Simplified Chinese, English, and Japanese.
